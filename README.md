
# AI and Social Influence

## Research question

How does information about a person's social
environment influence the persuasive strategies
selected by an AI model?

## Motivation

Social media persuasion often operates through
peer relationships, social consensus, and repeated
exposure. Private AI interactions introduce a
different setting for persuasion.

This project investigates how language models
adapt their persuasive strategies when given
information about a person's social environment.

## Research objectives

1. Compare model behavior across different
   social-context conditions.
2. Identify changes in argument selection,
   framing, and rhetorical strategies.
3. Evaluate whether these patterns are consistent
   across models and repeated experiments.
